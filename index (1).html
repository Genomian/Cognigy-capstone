Annual vehicle safety inspection

Most vehicles in Nexora must pass a safety inspection once every 12 months. Selected Qwik Service centers are certified official inspection stations.

What's checked

  • Brakes & parking brake
  • Headlights, signals & horn
  • Steering & suspension
  • Tires, wheels & rims
  • Mirrors, glass & wipers
  • Exhaust, fuel & emissions
  • Seats, belts & airbags
  • Doors, hood latch, floor pan & plate light

How it works

A certified inspector removes the old sticker and checks each safety item. If your vehicle passes, a new sticker is issued for your windshield. Driving without a valid sticker may lead to a ticket.

If it doesn't pass: you may receive a rejection notice. Repair the listed defects and return for reinspection within 15 days.

Safety inspection is separate from vehicle registration. Some localities also require emissions inspections.

Frequently asked questions

Quinn can answer these on the phone too.

What services are included in my booking?

The services you selected when booking. A technician may recommend extra work after inspecting your vehicle; some services depend on parts availability. Check your confirmation for final details.

Can I change my service center location?

Yes. You can usually change locations before final confirmation, though you may need to reselect available services or times.

Will I get a confirmation email or SMS?

Yes. A confirmation is typically sent by email, and optional text reminders may be available.

What time should I arrive for my service?

Arrive about 10–15 minutes early so check-in is quick and easy.

Are loaner vehicles available during service?

Loaner availability varies by location and service type. Check with your service center when booking, and ask about shuttle or waiting-area options.

Can I book if I'm not sure what my vehicle needs?

Yes. Choose a general inspection or diagnostic visit and discuss the symptoms with the technician.
